STANDALONE ATP-DEPENDENT AMIDE-LIGASE FAMILY INVENTORIES

  amp_forming_family_inventory.tex / .pdf / .png / .svg
  adp_forming_family_inventory.tex / .pdf / .png / .svg

The two figures contain 24 entries covering major relevant families, functional
groups and related substrate contexts. These are not 24 independent folds or
an exhaustive census of all ATP-utilizing enzymes. Entries intentionally expose
overlap, such as ATP-grasp subfamilies and the multiple domains in CphA1.

The AMP comparison includes ANL/McbA, GH3, NIS, AsnA, AsnB, AsnB-related
ring-forming ligases, DdaG and NAD synthetases. Related NRPS, aminoacyl-tRNA,
biotin-protein and lipoate-protein systems are explicitly marked.

The ADP comparison includes ATP-grasp peptide, isopeptide, glutathione/polyamine
and purine-pathway ligases, GS-fold enzymes, glutamate-cysteine ligases, Mur
ligases, FolC/FPGS, CphA1, and IaaL. Related protein/tRNA-bound systems are marked.
IaaL is included for its established ADP-forming reaction, without asserting a
fold assignment or treating nucleotide fate as proof of an experimentally
resolved intermediate. No fold assignment is asserted for DdaG here either.

Each row links to a primary study or authoritative enzyme/structure record.
family_inventory.json preserves the row content, references and build checks.
Source: build_family_tables.py. Rebuild from the repository root:
  python slides-candidacy/ligase-family-comparison/build_family_tables.py
