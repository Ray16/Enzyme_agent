30 — AMP-forming versus ADP-forming synthetases
Question: How does reaction mechanism change the modeled activation step?
Carboxylate activation releases PPi in the adenylation route, or ADP in the
phosphorylation route. During subsequent aminolysis, AMP or phosphate is the
leaving group, respectively. Overall ATP-derived products are AMP + PPi or
ADP + Pi. This table concerns carboxyl-activating amide ligases, not every
biochemical process that consumes ATP. NIS and GS-fold examples keep the table
relevant to this deck; ANL and ATP-grasp are not exhaustive class definitions.
Metal stoichiometry must be supported independently for each modeled enzyme.
Sources: Hollenhorst et al., Biochemistry 2009, doi:10.1021/bi9013165;
Mydy et al., Biochemistry 2020, PMCID:PMC7325057; Kurihara et al., JBC 2008,
PMID:18495664. Source links are embedded in the editable LaTeX table.

33 — Adaptive action selection
Question: What makes the framework adaptive instead of a fixed tool pipeline?
The current discovery protocol records hypotheses, predictions, source-linked
actions, observations and revisions. The model chooses scientific actions;
deterministic execution handles tool contracts and persistence. Bounded review
and resource limits can checkpoint unresolved work. A resource stop is not
evidence that search coverage is complete. The controller cannot independently
certify that an extracted passage entails the agent's scientific interpretation.
Source: enzyme_discovery_agent/README.md, Adaptive research decisions.

34 — Shared evidence and handoffs
Question: What is passed between agents, and how are conflicts handled?
The current schema-3.0 handoff separates admitted candidates, unresolved leads
and exclusions. Candidate identity, native evidence and modeling proposals have
different statuses. The receiving agent verifies identities and setup rather
than inheriting a validity claim from discovery. Fingerprints detect changed
bytes; they do not authenticate the scientific interpretation or prove activity.
Sources: component READMEs, Structural-modeling handoff sections.

36 — Model validation
Question: How can a convincing pose still fail to support a mechanistic claim?
Identity, sources, chemical setup, physical acceptance criteria, coverage and
provenance are checked separately. Failed input or pose validation returns to
the decision loop. A complete but unproductive prediction can motivate a new
scientific investigation; missing or stale outputs cannot. Geometry does not
estimate a catalytic barrier or prove target turnover. Generated samples are
technical model outputs, not biological replicates or a time trajectory.
Source: structural_modeling_agent/README.md, Scientific validation tools.
