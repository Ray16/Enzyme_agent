Smaller Boltz-2 ensembles for the experimental amide synthetase panel

The enrichment signal survives fewer poses. At 10 poses per enzyme, the mean
AUROC for the three strongest producers is 0.9166, compared with 0.9407 using
all 50. However, recovering all three in a fixed top-10 shortlist is sensitive
to sample count, especially because AS23 ranks ninth in the full-50 result.

The analysis uses 48 homologs with 50 matched one-Mg activation poses each.
For each enzyme, independently select a uniform random subset without
replacement. Repeat 10,000 times, using nested subsets across sample counts.
Keep the score fixed: A = sum(angle/distance) / sum(1/distance), larger first.
The strongest producers are AS9, AS17 and AS23. SfaB stays outside the ranking.
Harmonic distance is retained in the per-enzyme exports. No new cofolds ran.

A practical option on this panel is an initial 10 poses for every enzyme,
followed by completing the top 20 of 48 enzymes to 50 total poses and ranking
those retained enzymes by their completed score. This retrospective policy
recovers all three strongest producers in the final top 10 in 99.47% of trials,
and the exact full-50 top 10 in 98.13%. It uses 1,280 rather than 2,400 structures:
46.67% fewer structures, or an ideal sampling-stage throughput gain of 1.875x.
Startup and featurization overheads are excluded from that throughput estimate.
If completing any ensembles is undesirable, 15 poses and a top-15 shortlist
preserves all three strongest producers in 96.19% of the retrospective trials.

These probabilities describe the existing ensembles, not prospective success
rates on new enzymes. Agreement at 50 is guaranteed by construction. A separate
empirical bootstrap with replacement avoids the finite-population collapse:
even at 50 resampled poses, all three appear in the top 10 in only 70.32% of
5,000 trials. That sensitivity highlights uncertainty in the original reference
ranking; it is not a measured success rate for genuinely new cofolds.

All 14 prior descriptors were reproduced before resampling, and compared on
identical pose subsets. Weighted angle remains useful, but it is not uniquely
best across every endpoint: alternatives can tie or outperform it. This study
does not retune or replace the frozen metric. Assay labels remain fixed, and
10,000 pose draws do not add experimental replicates. The sample-budget results
apply to this one-Mg setup; transfer to two-Mg production cofolds remains untested.
